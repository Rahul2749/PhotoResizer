# Initial preset research

Checked on 25 October 2026. These are maintained file-rule aids, not official endorsements or guarantees of portal acceptance. Every app record includes its scope, cycle, source section, check date and next review date.

## Why these applications

The first batch covers national engineering, medical and university admissions, teaching/science eligibility, government recruitment and bank recruitment. It is a practical demand-based shortlist, **not a measured ranking of search volume**. No Google Trends, Keyword Planner or site search-query data was available in this research.

There is direct evidence of large applicant populations: NTA reports 1,604,854 unique JEE Main 2026 Paper 1 registrants across sessions, and approximately 22.79 lakh NEET UG 2026 registrants. These counts support prioritizing the two workflows; they do not establish demand for any particular resizer site.

- [NEET UG 2026 examination release, applicant counts](https://nta.ac.in/Download/Notice/Notice_20260502215114.pdf)

## Applied rules and limits

All KB values use this tool's disclosed decimal convention of 1,000 bytes per KB. The linked notices use KB/kb without defining a byte conversion. Bounds are treated as inclusive; users should follow the current portal if its checks differ. Files below a minimum are not padded.

| Option | Upload photo | Signature | Dimension handling / scope |
|---|---|---|---|
| JEE Main 2026 | JPG, 10–200 KB | JPG, 10–100 KB | Cited section does not prescribe fixed pixel dimensions; retain user's dimensions. Additional live capture on portal. |
| NEET UG 2026 | JPG, 10–200 KB | JPG, 10–100 KB | Retain dimensions; additional live capture. Other files and postcard/print requirements are outside this pair. |
| CUET UG 2026 | JPG, 10–200 KB | JPG, 10–50 KB | Retain dimensions; additional live capture. |
| CTET September 2026 | JPG, 10–100 KB | JPG, 3–30 KB | Notice specifies 3.5 × 4.5 cm / 3.5 × 1.5 cm. App derives 413 × 531 / 413 × 177 px at an explicitly chosen 300 DPI, including JPEG density metadata. Pixel rounding gives approximate physical measurements. |
| Joint CSIR-UGC NET June 2026 | JPG, 10–200 KB | JPG, 10–50 KB | Retain dimensions. This is distinct from UGC-NET; do not mix the two exams' notices. |
| IBPS 2026–27 calendar guide | JPEG, 20–50 KB | JPEG, 10–20 KB | General calendar guidance only. Does not supply pixel sizes. Specific recruitment notice still controls; live capture is also listed. |
| SBI PO / Junior Associate FAQ guide | 20–50 KB | 10–20 KB | General official FAQ preferred sizes 200 × 230 / 140 × 60 px. JPG is the tool's export choice. No current recruitment cycle is claimed. Check current advertisement for format, scanning resolution and live capture. |
| SSC CGL 2026 signature guide | Live capture on SSC portal | JPG, 10–20 KB | Only signature is counted as an upload. Paragraph 9.6 gives about 6 × 2 cm while Annexure IV gives 4 × 2 cm. Dimensions intentionally not applied; current portal must resolve the conflict. |

## Publication rules

Do not silently reuse a previous cycle for a new year. Display the cited cycle even after its application window closes. Do not infer preferred pixel dimensions from unrelated bank notices. A newly published notice or corrigendum takes precedence over a generic FAQ/calendar.

The picker disables automatic application after the stored review date until a maintainer rechecks the source. The initial review is due 24 November 2026; no scheduled automation is configured. Source pages open only on an explicit click, in a separate tab. Applying presets does not send selected files or filenames to those sites.

Omitted for now: PAN/passport variants, RRB-specific recruitment rules, a blanket UGC-NET preset, thumb impressions, declarations and certificates. They need distinct, complete source review and appropriate document workflows. In particular, UGC-NET December 2026's extracted text did not establish format as clearly as the independently reviewed CSIR bulletin; it was not inferred from the June UGC-NET replica, whose signature range differs from prose.
